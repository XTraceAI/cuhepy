## What we now know

The new executable controls separate three costs previously easy to conflate:

1. **Trusted preparation:** ordinary encrypted queries remove fresh encrypted
   owner answers, but point proofs need hints, dense gates need large retained
   fingerprints, and the seed-affine gate needs a trusted seed factory.
2. **Original-query binding:** verifying index multiplication on a supplied
   expanded ciphertext does not verify its relation to the original query.
   Canonical client expansion works but duplicates costly seed circuitry.
3. **Whole useful request:** actual received-index/answer/query RPC controls
   include owner preparation, verification and secret decoding. Even retained
   global N2048 profiles lose to authorized caches on the two finite datasets.

The E77 identity is a useful interface: for fixed original C1, every gadget
carry/key-switch offset is independent of query-bearing C0. Compile the latter
to at most N inputs. However, the literal private factory still holds2hN
fingerprints and computes every fresh seed offset. It is not an escape from
the user's earlier question about duplicating work at a controlled verifier.

## The next constructive research question

**Can the canonical seed-dependent circuit be certified/generated with less
total trusted work/state while the small query-bearing gate remains safe?**

A potentially relevant restricted result would define a class of structured
encrypted operators and a typed release relation, give a new binding or
generation transformation for that class, and prove the full rejected-response
composition. Existing packing, affine hoisting, Freivalds, generic vFHE/ring
proofs or an ordinary GPU kernel are attributed ingredients. The new step must
be something those constructions do not already supply.

Two concrete competitors remain: a carry-aware admissible outer construction
(which must price compiled H-prime, not only implicit D) and a succinct public
certificate for canonical seed offsets/expanded input. Give each a bounded
construction card before implementing a proof system. It needs a genuine
security dimension/commitment, canonical digit/range constraints, all branch
and context bindings, and fresh public challenges or a justified hidden-state
feedback theorem. Tiny unbound points or a toy insecure commitment are not a
prototype of the required assurance.

The literal E77 private-beta factory is the fallback discriminator, not a free
oracle in the new construction. Full query/response/key/setup/private bytes,
trusted/public work, rejection semantics and useful cache comparison decide
selection. If the proposed step is only a known generic wrapper or its best
optimistic complete cost loses, return to R4/R5 without new native/CUDA work.

## Proof and evaluation obligations if a candidate survives

First prove an exact integer/field relation and an aggregate first-false-accept
bound under ideal hidden challenges. Then define the complete owner/client
and malicious-server game, including index/seed/key enrollment, every response
and acceptance bit, outputs, lifetime updates and permitted leakage. Separate
the HE/related-key hybrid, seeded randomness, certificate binding/extraction,
owner authenticity and conditional verification transitions. The reduction
must use the same receiver boundary as the code. Timing and durable freshness
remain implementation obligations, not consequences of an algebra lemma.

Select one primary objective and held-out workload after that finite screen.
Use the actual strong same-harness controls; never exclude the owner's retained
data. Close private bootstrap/independent-resource uncertainty before deployment
claims and use justified parameters before performance/security comparisons.
Existing company speedups remain valuable regardless of the paper outcome.

**Gate decision:** no entire P-package is accepted. R0's bounded premise task
stays complete, R2/R3/R4/R6 remain open, R5/R7 and new native/CUDA acceleration
remain inactive until their scoped conditions hold. Gates B/C/D do not pass
because several exact control experiments completed.
