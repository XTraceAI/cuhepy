## Exact completion, observables, and prohibited conclusions

A completed census requires one validated exact count for every canonical
representative in the frozen inventory, zero missing/duplicate records, and all
1820 subsets represented once with their orbit weights. Each count is odd,
includes the zero secret exactly once, and has denominator **`3^16`**. Every
structured `X^4-a` packet must have count one, with the existing degree-four
norm/divisor proof as an independent known control. Any failure stops the census
as failed validation; it is not a new extremality result.

Only on full completion report the global maximum mass, ties/maximizing orbit
families, the full collection of nonstructured violating classes, count histogram
by orbit size/packet label, and the exact orbit-weighted sum

`Z4 = sum_orbits |orbit| * count_orbit / 3^16`.

This is **E[binom(k(S),4)]**, where k(S) is the number of vanishing roots. It is not
the probability that S has at least four vanishing roots: witnesses/events can
overlap between subsets. A standard union upper bound may be stated explicitly as
such, with zero handled once:

`Pr(k(S)>=4) <= min(1, [1 + sum_orbits |orbit|*(count_orbit-1)] / 3^16)`.

Do not present this bound as an exact union count or a sharp rank-defect bound.
Do not enumerate a full secret-to-root-set law under this contract. Partial
outputs may list only validated individual observations and coverage/resources;
they must not carry a global maximum, complete violating-class list, or completed
weighted-sum claim. Separate reused E122 visits, fresh E123 visits, extraction
visits, vector updates, and input-validation/metadata work in the ledger.
