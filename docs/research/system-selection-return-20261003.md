## System direction and precise contribution gap

Retain the homemade CUDA/BGV evaluator as the principal HE engineering
foundation, with BFV and Paillier fallbacks and SEAL as a labeled control.
This choice does not select a production parameter set or prove that HE beats
permitted client caching. Distinct owner-index/layout profiles are separate
competitors with different premises; the ordinary public-index BGV row is not
the strongest result from every earlier BGV experiment.

The plausible system research question is now a **complete, paid safe-release
frontier for the native RNS evaluation graph**: can a specific graph or boundary
algorithm preserve GPU throughput while establishing every terminal byte with
less trusted work and internal traffic than the strongest matching alternatives?
This is a candidate question, not an originality claim. Naming a TEE or applying
Freivalds checks does not supply the missing contribution. A surviving answer
must identify a distinct transformation and establish its effect over an equally
specialized Slalom-style checker and the other controls.

More precisely, the missing candidate is a source-exact canonical CRT/digit and
terminal-rounding relation or compressed certificate that reduces both trusted
work and total protected traffic/state. No such prior-separated relation is
proved here. Fixed-map adjoints, cached enrollment, ordinary hoisting, generic
layerwise checks and the present product partition remain equally informed
controls. A weighted DAG checksum by itself is not a new contribution.

The next construction must first implement a complete native reference of the
now-working tiny invariant and a fully trusted native replay control. It must
pay canonical full-Q digits, local c2, every prime, rotation, tail, compact
conversion and all response positions. An unchecked complete GPU answer cannot
be signed because an earlier product stage was checked. No production private
decoder should be exposed to this experimental controller before its enrollment,
receipt and admission boundary has been separately reviewed.

## Finite construction handoff

1. **Define one complete native boundary and its controls before a build.** Use
   the existing graph and terminal format, explicit owner registration, original
   query and snapshot identity. Freeze exact outputs and allowed state. Derive a
   cost ledger for a secretless native checker in two complete modes: checked
   product/trusted suffix and prepared full native replay;
   reject a product-only design if its paid continuation already defeats its
   intended operating point. Layerwise checking is an alternative known control,
   with its additional output traffic and canonical conversions charged.
2. **Build and validate that one native composition.** Preserve the Python/GMP
   controller as the oracle; use the archived native packets, independently
   generated held-out queries, partial groups and adversarial mutations at every
   boundary. Keep admission separate from the external expected-output comparator.
   Charge checker preprocessing, entropy and all retained state.
3. **Measure a complete matched frontier before a service.** Include all relevant
   E16 BGV controls, BFV, strongest Paillier hybrid, full replay, specialized
   randomized delegation, applicable proof admission, plaintext protected search
   and permitted raw/compressed/mutable client caches. Include protected-boundary
   transfer, setup, fresh-client acquisition, updates and returning clients.
   Missing adaptations are unknown costs, not evidence that those controls lose.
4. **Select or stop one prior-separated claim.** Require a named transformation,
   complete correctness/conditional soundness statement and useful operating
   point against those controls. If only known integration survives, retain it
   as company engineering and an evaluation artifact; do not claim a new theorem
   or a conference-ready original system. Do not resume a toy scan grid merely
   because this comparison is negative.
5. **Only then implement the protected service and security argument.** Bound
   global block attempts and owner epochs durably; bind graph, keys, index, query,
   position and complete response; handle rollback, replay, concurrency, entropy,
   receipt parsing and attestation. Evaluate actual protected transport and
   signing. Prove the conditional no-false-admission composition, then separately
   establish HE correctness/privacy, concrete parameters and private side-channel
   assurance. The current RTX3080 supplies no confidential-GPU deployment claim.

This handoff is a finite construction sequence. It is not authorization for an
automatic new benchmark, backend, parameter sweep, source census or paper claim.
The next expensive component requires its own exact useful-target specification,
resource cap and independent review. Venue choice does not change these gates.

## What the current evidence cannot establish

The matched fixture uses unequal, unapproved HE profiles and different client
hardware roles. It measures local arithmetic and locally generated legitimate
responses, not WAN latency, safe malicious-server release, attestation or a
deployed freshness protocol. One process/key block with five queries supplies
paired fixture ratios and ranges, not population confidence intervals.

Owner key/index preparation is not fresh-client key/manifest acquisition.
The cache update pays a full snapshot reseal; a stronger authenticated mutable
or compressed cache remains a required control before an HE update advantage.
Full client caching is allowed by the deployment contract. It must not be
forbidden or burdened with invented constraints to create an outsourcing win.

No original main mechanism or complete protocol-security result is accepted by
these bounded components. A production conclusion still requires independent
cryptographic review in addition to the construction and paid evaluation above.
