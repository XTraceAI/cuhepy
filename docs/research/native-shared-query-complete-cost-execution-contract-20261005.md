## Functionality and inputs

Use the one retained public binary corpus
`research-data/q76-source-scale-20261004/source-cohort1/public-binary-rows.bin`:
32,768 rows, 64 little-endian bytes per row. Counts are exactly 8,224, 16,384 and
32,768. Bind UInt64 maximum minus ordinal as each ID. Output every exact Hamming
distance and top3 ordered by `(distance, original ordinal)`.

The eight query words are `55` repeated 64 times, `a3` repeated 64 times, then
64-byte SHAKE256 outputs from `cuhepy/Q77/public-query/v1` and the eight-byte
little-endian indices 2 through 7. Every trajectory uses those same plaintext
words. Its encrypted requests use fresh owner entropy and distinct bound
nonces. The update changes exactly ordinals 0 through 31 to alternating
query0/query1 XOR one. It preserves IDs/shape and advances logical authority once.

The homemade owner-origin canonical30 profile is fixed: N16,384, dimension512,
t1031, eta21, P33,548,413 and the exact Q/two ordered primes in the JSON.
Correctness support checks are not cryptographic parameter approval. No larger
ring, changed sampler, modulus grid, CUDA or Nsight execution is authorized here.

## Event and arrival law

The full rotate/reverse order is fixed in the JSON: all six calibration blocks
first, then twelve held-out blocks, with all six trajectories in each block.
Each lifetime records its attempt before setup. Initial signed uploads traverse
the actual shared client upload lane; the public assembler publishes the pinned
complete input. Remote preparation and owner descriptor acquisition occur
before query arrivals. Cache-only setup uploads its AEAD snapshot and does not
download HE keys, index or descriptor.

Returning cache acquires its cache before its excluded warmup. Fresh cache warms
only the public one-row scan code, with no acquisition; the first measured
arrival precedes authenticated acquisition. Remote/prefetch warmups execute one
actual encrypted query using query0. Prefetch starts actual acquisition at the
first measured query and races the fixed allowed remote path; both share one
download lane. Retain and join every loser and acquisition before closing remote
use or starting its cache-only update.

For each of the eight measured queries, arrival is the actual monotonic time
after the previous independent oracle and resource check. Completion is actual
owner receipt authentication, at-most-once private consumption, complete
decoding/cache scan and top3 selection. The independent all-distance oracle runs
after completion and before the next arrival. It cannot be substituted with
sampling or included in the measured owner completion. A failed oracle stops
the cohort and preserves its scratch.

The **primary metric is the arithmetic mean of completion minus arrival** under
this law, equally weighting the two keys, counts and eight requests. The remote
initial upload/preparation and excluded warmup are pre-arrival costs. Report
them separately, as well as first-answer-from-lifetime-start, whole lifetime and
update-to-next-answer; do not describe the primary mean as including cold setup.
The returning and fresh cache states are separate named comparisons. This is a
fresh public-worker lifetime with local retained owner keys, not a measured
cross-device key-provisioning or already-warm-cloud deployment.

After the eight observations and joined prefetch work, record the complete paid
