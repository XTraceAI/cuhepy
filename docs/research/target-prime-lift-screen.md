canonical source0<=c<Q, output0<=y<P and integer |r|<Q/2. Check

`tr ≡ -Pc (mod p0 and p1); Qy ≡ tr (mod P)`.

Because t is a unit moduloQ, the first equations uniquely pin one centered r.

half ties unreachable. Neither proof nor predicate authenticates the source
