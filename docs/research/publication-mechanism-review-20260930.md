## What remains creative and falsifiable

The surviving question is the **verification/release construction**, rather
than another arithmetic optimization of the old circuit. Two precise avenues
remain for R3:

1. A carry-aware structured outer packing/registration construction. It must
   handle the genuine bounded gadget/rounding operations; H=D A and Z=C D
   alone are insufficient. Compare known coefficient-norm packing, not only
   full-width matrices. Show exact owner binding and admissible correctness.
2. A certificate for public **ciphertext arithmetic**, verified before any HE
   secret is used. This can bypass an outer plaintext-matrix layer, but requires
   fresh encrypted queries, all arithmetic stages and their costs. A generic
   SNARG wrapper is known and is not the proposed original contribution.

The second avenue has a close comparator:
[Huang et al., *Fully Homomorphic Encryption with Efficient Public Verification*,
§1.1–1.3](https://eprint.iacr.org/2024/1764.pdf) gives ring R1CS, sum-check and
ring polynomial commitments, including non-ring gadget/modulus operations.
Its stated remaining efficiency question includes linear-time preprocessing
and proof generation. This is targeted reading, not theorem review or an
implemented baseline. Our earlier vFHE/HELIOPOLIS cards remain relevant.

## E70 proposal: exact quotient certificate before a full proof system

Start with a narrow coefficient-field identity. For degree-<N polynomials,

```
sum_j a_j(X) b_j(X) = c(X) + (X^N + 1) h(X) over F_Q[X].
```

Here c has degree <N and h degree <=N-2. General field-point testing uses
ordinary polynomial evaluation and the quotient. Testing only NTT roots is
unsafe against deliberately concentrated frequency errors. Plain evaluation
without h is also incorrect away from those roots. These are independently
testable controls, not a new polynomial-identity theorem.

**First oracle, preregistered before measurement:** full-product versus
negacyclic/quotient reconstruction; exact root-concentrated false response;
exhaustive small-field acceptance sets; exposed/reused-point counterexample;
bounded degree/field/lifetime counts and exact source/raw hashes. Test the
known cyclic+negacyclic decomposition as a quotient-generation control.

A verifier could retain secret points and evaluations of fixed generators
instead of whole coefficient challenge adjoints. This trades private state for
a quotient polynomial and a degree-dependent soundness bound. Points must
remain hidden and the whole accept/abort transcript must be argued; publishing
and reusing them permits interpolation forgeries. No key/noise/privacy claim
follows from the identity. Publicly sampled points instead require commitment
and opening order with input/quotient binding.

**Promotion gate:** compare a complete secret-point/private-hint protocol or
public commitment construction to dense Freivalds, original ring proofs and
direct BGV, under the same lifetime target. Charge quotient generation,
encrypted-query upload, proof/reply, hidden setup, failure semantics and client
work. If the wire cost or setup just relocates the old bottleneck, keep the
negative control and stop. No new native/GPU mechanism before this gate.

This adds a bounded R3 alternative; it does not remove R1/R2 obligations, replace
the main exact-score contract, silently implement encrypted queries, or declare
R6 acceptance. The eventual point of invention must be a new safe composition
or proof algorithm with a real full-cost margin beyond these known identities.

versions. Compare full costs and reaction semantics before native/CUDA work.
