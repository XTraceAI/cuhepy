## A. Discriminating source budget

The seeded owner encryption has phase `m+t*e`, with `|m|<=floor(t/2)` and
independent centered-binomial errors of support `[-eta,eta]`. Its public key
error is not an additional encryption term. Ordinary public-key encryption
has a different bound and remains a separately labeled control.

Expand the depth-one sum into message products, two message/error cross terms
and error products over the integer negacyclic ring. Separate the final
centered plaintext, its integer carry and the resulting BGV error. Exhaust
N2/t3/eta1 message/error supports and exhibit an attaining worst-case input;
this is a support/correctness screen, not an estimate of its probability.

Recompute original minimal-correctness Q and new NTT primes above 4P, 6P and
8P, where P is the independently derived centered phase bound. The same
error support gives a valid correctness bound in these changed contexts;
this says nothing about their RLWE hardness. Charge new keys/index and added
bits. Record both ordinary nearest-message half margins and the stricter
signed odd-LUT quarter margins. Review a known full-domain method's actual
domain and bootstrap requirements; do not silently adapt its even-plaintext
theorem to our odd t or charge its auxiliary keys as zero.

Stop tuning switching against a negative source budget. A changed Q is a
correctness-valid but security-unapproved context, not a parameter recommendation.

## B. Conditional law, exact oracles and accepted feedback

Fix the original query/index-derived score family, prescribed signs, output
coefficients and pre-PBS moduli before a fresh target secret and independent
switching-key mask/error coins. The output mask must use source digits and
key masks only. The source secret/input may be dependent. The public key
bodies and the posterior target secret are not assumed independent.

For an event, aggregate coefficients of each shared random variable before
taking squares. A uniform independent ternary secret has variance proxy 1;
a centered-binomial eta error has proxy eta/2. Thus for numerator
`Z=w*T+B*sum(d_l*E_l)`, use
`2V=2*sum(w_i^2)+eta*B^2*sum(d_l,i^2)`.
With K fixed allowed events and `L=kappa+ceil_log2(2K)`, the integer bound
`ceil_sqrt(2V*L)` has whole-family failure at most 2^-kappa. This is a known
MGF/union control, not a Gaussian or variance-only estimate. Add a worst-case
body rounding term and an original-source S-squared residual bound separately.

Exhaust a complete tiny scalar source/target/mask/centered-binomial coin law,
including reused errors, both signs and two stages. This small-modulus toy is
an algebra/dependency oracle, not a secure or necessarily decryptable HE
instance. Compare joint versus posterior independence. Also check whole small
ring target/error supports and one fresh valid homemade BGV differential.

Use exact rational convolution to compare a fixed 32-coordinate weight with
the invalid post-key choice `w_i=sign(T_i)`, under an explicitly revealing
toy transcript. Include the entire 2^32 sign family as the correct control.
This is a falsifier for a removed ordering premise, not an attack on RLWE or a
published scheme. Compare postselection within an approved family to the
whole-family event. No retries or new inputs are approved by elapsed time.

State a conditional accepted-release coupling: absent original-relation proof
failure and the whole-family noise event, release equals the approved ideal
function/feedback. Sum actual epoch failure budgets; repeated subset selection
does not create fresh noise trials. Public/private work order, key provenance,
key cycles and upstream/PBS relations remain explicit premises. The statistical
lemma alone is not an HE confidentiality reduction or side-channel assurance.
