## The result worth building toward

Build a homemade BGV exact-search service with independently checked execution
plans, then establish **which verification placements remain useful after
paying for their arithmetic, internal traffic, state, updates and acquisition**.
The default prospective systems paper combines complete supporting assurance
with a prior-separated, reproducible deployment finding. A formal/security
framing instead requires a substantive assurance result beyond routine
composition. One main result is enough; the supporting components need not
each be original. Fast ciphertext evaluation alone is already
well represented in the literature.

Our specific question is whether a complete certificate can connect the
owner's encoding and noise premises to malicious-response admission and fresh
client release, while exposing useful choices between checking, protected
execution and retained state. The research gap is **provisional**. A compatible
composition of existing verification, type-checking and protected-execution
methods is the first counterconstruction to attempt, not an inferior baseline.

The three claim cards remain one proposed system:

## Evidence that changes the build decision

The old 32k panel selects homemade BGV as an engineering foundation: its local
median is 117.298 ms, versus BFV 892.813 ms and Paillier lookup CUDA 2,599.103 ms.
Those are experimental profiles and a different earlier graph, with network
and complete protected admission excluded and setup qualifications retained.
The permitted returning cache is 4.384 ms in that panel. These measurements
cannot establish equal-security or secure-service speedups for the new graph.

The selected native graph now has source-scale correctness, prepared replay
and exact aggregate controls. The [aggregate return](native-shared-query-aggregate-20261004.md)
preserves 22 retained cases, 394,944 aggregate coefficient comparisons, 360
selected tests and 5,568 coefficient/isolated-frequency faults. Its source-scale
reference is recovered from an independently GMP-validated saved trace; it is
not a new encryption/decryption experiment.

For 32,768 records, the full-trace internal body is 127,057,920 B; the exact
aggregate body is 1,474,560 B; the complete compact frame is 204,895 B. These
are different interfaces. The aggregate control still pays genuine protected
query expansion, three products per feature and the terminal suffix, as well
as an untrusted producer. Prepared original-request-only execution needs no
producer or aggregate witness. Delegation therefore has no established
arithmetic advantage over this control. Measure it rather than assuming that
the approximately 86-fold internal body reduction implies a latency gain.

The native prepared public cache is 423,624,704 B for one group and
826,277,888 B for two, before other state and scratch. The same 32k owner data
occupies only 2,097,152 B as packed vectors, plus 262,144 B of UInt64 IDs. That
contrast makes device acquisition, public preparation and snapshot lifetimes
central to the scientific question. It supplies no reason to forbid caching.

The accumulated negative results also constrain the plan. Generic gadget
propagation, shared expansion, delayed switching, operator state and affine
updates remain credited prior methods. The E101/E110/Q57/Q59/Q74/Q75
containment decisions are not reopened by renaming them. No broad ring-size,
radix, Fourier or lookup-table grid is on the critical path.

## The formal work starts with a small, explicit specification

Specify `Search(snapshot, query)` as the complete ordered vector of exact
Hamming distances, zero tails and top-k by `(distance, original row ordinal)`,
returning the corresponding bound IDs. Test reversed IDs in the new cache and
certificate controls; the older cache's numeric-ID rule is a different
contract that SB01's positional IDs did not expose. Keep
owner origin/sampler support as explicit premises. A signature authenticates
bytes; it does not prove those premises or a security parameter estimate.

The certificate judgment should expose:

```text
origin/key/snapshot
    -> reference graph and public phase induction
    -> canonical common-Q values and both actual-prime representations
    -> complete admitted aggregate or trace
    -> exact terminal conversion and complete frame
    -> current request-bound authorization
    -> at-most-once claimed private release
```

Draft and mechanize the arithmetic and abstract state lemmas in one proof
assistant, with an independently executable certificate checker. Lean 4 is the
initial mathematical-model target; freeze its version/dependencies before proof
implementation. Do not describe a model proof as verification of the complete
C++ binary, operating system or hardware. Native refinement and deployed
freshness remain separately stated obligations.

The deliverables are: (1) full-ring admission from common-Q parsing and both
invertible NTTs; (2) exact output from the selected origin and public phase
induction, including the actual Q-to-P codec; (3) the request/snapshot/frame
authorization invariant under an explicit crash/freshness model; and
(4) conditional confidentiality composition with public admission feedback.
The final reduction must name augmented evaluation-key/KDM assumptions,
authentication/hash failure, owner-origin failure, admission/correctness
failure, freshness/TEE failure and permitted/private leakage. It must not claim
that plain IND-CPA makes raw BGV CCA secure. Private-key timing and sampler
assurance are independent of the public arithmetic gate.

For randomized delegation, freeze the adversarial attempt budget and statistical
target separately from HE security. The existing degree-two component-check
card has prospective lifetime error `B*(2/min(p_i))^r` under committed claims
and fresh hidden independent challenges. One-prime errors prevent multiplying
limb probabilities. With the selected roughly 60-bit primes, strong lifetime
amplification can require as many aggregate products as exact paired checking.
This is accounting within that known probe family, not a universal lower bound
on verification or a new randomized primitive. An unfinished randomized
adapter requires a deterministic-class headline, not a claimed defeat of vFHE.

## One more creative variant, conditional on a measured reason

Keep the existing mixed-cut, streaming and multi-object options in the execution
plan. They require a paid bottleneck and give compatible controls the same
optimizations. A more substantial changed premise is worth recording:
**the query alone may be confidentially provisioned to a protected evaluator,
while the plaintext index and HE decryption key stay outside it.**

For binary data, `distance_i = sum_j (q_j + (1-2*q_j)*b_ij)`. With query bits
known inside that boundary, an encrypted feature-major index can use ordinary
plaintext-weighted HE sums, removing encrypted-query expansion and tensor
multiplication from that separate graph. An untrusted GPU must not receive
unmasked query weights. Any masking/delegation wrapper requires an exact
statement, fresh-mask/challenge and snapshot lifetime argument, all-coordinate
checking, paid preprocessing and a proof that the terminal phase stays valid.
Slalom, EMVP and ordinary encrypted linear analytics are strong controls;
their masking/preprocessing is credited, not repackaged as a new primitive.

This variant **changes the query trust contract** and introduces a confidential
query in the protected CPU, so Argos's no-secret-in-CPU reasoning cannot simply
be copied. It is not selected or implemented here, and it does not reopen
failed reusable-adjoint laws. Register at most one feasibility task only if
Q77 identifies protected query expansion/aggregation as the decisive cost and
a real deployment accepts this changed custody. First compare the strongest
known linear/delegation construction and derive every-premise bounds; stop the
new mechanism claim if it is contained. No new HE/grid/timing starts from this
memo alone.

## Paper and artifact shape

The paper should state the owner/trust/output contract first; show the complete
cost problem; specify the certificate and its theorem/assumptions; describe the
homemade runtime and legal placements; then report the paid operating frontier
and ablations. Required figures compare evaluator-only versus complete cost,
internal versus client links, setup/device/update lifetimes, protected memory,
and cache/replay wins as well as any positive region. Security claims have a
separate assumption/implementation table.

The artifact contains source-pinned commands, exact profile/input laws,
preregistered workloads, raw observations, failure/qualification receipts,
independent references and restoration manifests. The closest-work comparison
distinguishes mathematical containment, same-contract adaptations, different
trust/output contracts and unknown reproduction costs. Do not use cross-paper
hardware ratios or omit an inconvenient control.
