share a cheaper relation for our coefficient/CRT layout than doing a generic

1. Original coefficient layout to TFHE-style extracted scores/key-value LUTs.

   selection. Charge changed t/Q, new keys/index, residency, checks and setup.
3. An authenticated bounded-score lookup/coverage relation with extension-field
   challenges. Bind every original encrypted-score dependency and integer
   multiplicity, then compare a strong standard lookup relation, not an
   under-specified base-field product.

The proposed new step must be stated before the oracle: for example a shared
trace/carry witness or specialized bounded-score relation with a demonstrably
cheaper complete interface. Reusing known Frobenius traces, sample extraction,
grand products or counting sort alone is insufficient. Do not implement all
three full systems hoping that their composition itself becomes a contribution.

Required independent negatives: component/RNS substitution, integer-count
alias, wrong ID/tie, omitted-score coverage, challenge-before-binding,
server-known threshold leakage, incompatible characteristic, missing noise
bound and private-key work before accepted original-input binding.
