## Creative successor: actual deterministic quantizer law

The bounded successor review selects a **theorem gate with unknown novelty**,
not another compiler. E113 charges the codec's full worst-case support. A unit
secret and genuinely uniform fresh mask make the whole c0 vector uniform and
independent of fresh CBD errors, giving an exact deterministic codec-error law.
That identity and ordinary concentration are standard controls. The possible
contribution would have to be a new useful dependency/sampler argument for the
actual protocol, including adaptive pre-mask queries, reused maintenance keys,
every admitted common-integer witness and full compact terminal release.

[Q40/E114](query-quantizer-law-screen.md) tested only the existing
N16384/d512/count8192/t1031/CBD21/radix15 profile at drop22 anddrop23, under
explicitly ideal uniform-mask/unit-secret premises. Complete conditional
statistical lifetime bounds are2^-2722 and2^-2595. The strongest generic gets
the identical law and pointwise maintenance bound, ratio1: the literal known
recipe stops. Query packet MODEL100,460→98,412 B, response MODEL131,164 B
unchanged:2,048 B saved, about0.884% of complete query+response. No measured
latency or20% complete-cost target follows. These statistical terms do not
set actual SHAKE, secret-law, authentication or deployment failure probability.

The real SHAKE seed is public. It cannot be replaced in the joint seed/output
transcript by an independent information-theoretically uniform million-bit
vector. A follow-up [Q41/E115 mathematical card](query-mask-marginal-correctness-card-20261003.md)
corrects the earlier broader warning: a **marginal correctness-only** reduction
can use an efficient seed-blind sufficient event covering every server choice,
then stop before revealing the unknown challenge seed. Its ordinary conditional
bound is setup failure plus the sum of ideal bad-event, sampler-truncation and
PRG distinguishing terms. Concrete SHAKE/entropy advantage, nonunit-secret
probability and the actual all-witness protocol implication remain unknown.
This is not a joint-transcript privacy proof or new cryptographic primitive.
The [single stream-budget card](query-mask-stream-budget-card-20261003.md)
also computes a standard exact uniform-stream truncation bound below2^-186
over2^32 queries with a fixed131,120-byte budget. The runtime sampler remains
uncapped/unchanged. This assigns neither actual PRG advantage nor unit-key
probability, and makes no claim of186-bit system security.
Current [BGV dependency analysis](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.18597v3) and the
[average-case critique](https://eprint.iacr.org/2025/1036) are direct comparison
obligations, already preserved in the literature archive. A different application
scope alone does not establish a new mathematical mechanism.

The ideal frontier fact survives as a known control. **Q41/E115 remains partial**:

The marginal card's prospective drop22/drop23 gate is historical: E114 has now
